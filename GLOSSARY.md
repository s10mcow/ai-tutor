# AI Tutor

An AI tutor for two homeschooled children, initially aged 10 and 12, focused on developing their thinking and problem-solving through subjects they explore.

## Language

**Learner**:
A child who works with the AI tutor to learn.
_Avoid_: User (when referring specifically to a child learning with the tutor)

**Tutor**:
The AI teaching partner that helps a learner discover what to learn and work through learning activities using questions, hints, and explanations.
_Avoid_: Answer bot

**Independent learning**:
Learning in which a child works with the tutor without requiring a parent to choose the topic or participate in the session.

**Critical thinking**:
The ability to ask useful questions, explain reasons, check evidence, consider alternatives, and revise conclusions when warranted. This includes questioning the tutor's answers.

**AI literacy**:
The knowledge and skills involved in using AI. It is one subject learners can study with the tutor, rather than the main purpose of every activity.

**Learning evidence**:
A learner's explanation, solution, or creation that demonstrates their understanding and reasoning. Learners show this evidence to their parent themselves.
_Avoid_: Activity log (time spent or messages exchanged alone do not demonstrate learning)

**Portfolio**:
A learner's collection of learning evidence, including attempts, discoveries, and written or spoken reflections in their own words. The learner chooses which work to show their parent.
