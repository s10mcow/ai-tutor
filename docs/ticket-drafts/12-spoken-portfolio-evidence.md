# 12: Deliberately save a spoken Portfolio reflection

**What to build:** A Learner explicitly records or selects a spoken reflection for their Portfolio and can later play it while showing their work. Ordinary conversation remains separate from permanent recording.

**Blocked by:** 09 — Talk and type within the same coaching session; 10 — Save and show Learning evidence in a Portfolio.

**Status:** accepted for implementation; published issue linked in [tracker index](../tracker-index.json).

**Source stories:** 49, 50, 51, 55.

- [ ] The interface distinguishes speaking to the Tutor from deliberately recording a reflection to keep.
- [ ] The Learner can review the proposed reflection and choose to save or discard it before it becomes a lasting Portfolio asset.
- [ ] A saved spoken reflection appears with its Portfolio entry and can be played during a later visit.
- [ ] Discarded recordings and ordinary voice turns do not become permanent application audio assets; provider retention follows the separately verified policy.
- [ ] Saved recordings, playback, and any derived text remain associated with the correct Learner and protected against unauthorized retrieval.
- [ ] Save retries do not duplicate the entry, and a failed recording or save leaves a clear recoverable state.
- [ ] Browser scenarios verify explicit save and later playback, discard, ordinary speech without permanent saving, and sibling isolation using controlled audio fixtures.

**Demo:** Speak a reflection, review and save it, return later to play it, and demonstrate that a separate ordinary conversation was not archived as audio.

**Scope:** A new explicit recording is sufficient; saving an arbitrary earlier voice turn is not required if that would need default raw-audio retention. Picture Portfolio entries are not a blocker.
