# 04: Discover and choose learning activities

**What to build:** The Tutor helps a Learner identify an interest and choose a concrete activity. The Learner can explore biking, discuss art, ask for maths help, or learn about AI without their initial preferences becoming a fixed track.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Complete a typed coaching exchange.

**Status:** accepted for implementation; published issue linked in [tracker index](../tracker-index.json).

**Source stories:** 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17.

- [ ] Opening a new learning session offers a brief interest-discovery interaction and concrete, selectable activities rather than requiring a parent-written lesson.
- [ ] Selecting an activity opens the coaching session with its purpose visible and available to the Tutor.
- [ ] Biking exploration, art exploration, and maths help can be selected separately. Declining a proposed subject connection is respected.
- [ ] A Learner can reject the suggestions, introduce a new interest, and receive an appropriate activity without changing their profile's age or pretending to be their sibling.
- [ ] AI literacy is available as a topic but does not become the compulsory framing of every session.
- [ ] Starting preferences can inform suggestions without imposing a grade, curriculum, fixed track, or parent intervention on each choice.
- [ ] Browser scenarios verify discovery, selection, a declined connection, a new interest, and independent entry into the chosen coaching activity using controlled Tutor responses.

**Demo:** Choose biking exploration, decline a maths connection, then start a different art or AI-literacy activity.

**Scope:** Provide the activity-choice flow that a short game can extend. It does not implement game mechanics or long-term memory; a functional text exploration is enough to complete this slice.
