# 08: Learn from workbook and artwork pictures

**What to build:** A Learner uploads a picture of a workbook question or paper artwork, reviews what the Tutor understood, corrects it if necessary, and continues with coaching or art discussion based on the confirmed picture.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Connect model access with visible usage controls.

**Status:** draft — awaiting breakdown approval and tracker configuration.

**Source stories:** 12, 14, 15, 23, 24, 25, 26, 61.

- [ ] A Learner can select a supported image from the computer and see it attached to the correct learning session.
- [ ] Image assets are scoped to the authenticated family and Learner, including direct asset access and profile switching.
- [ ] The Tutor presents its interpretation before acting on it. The Learner can confirm or correct a misread number, instruction, or description.
- [ ] Confirmed workbook content feeds the small-step coaching flow; confirmed artwork feeds an art discussion without a compulsory maths connection.
- [ ] An unreadable, unsupported, or failed upload produces a clear correction or retry path and does not trigger invented image details.
- [ ] Provider calls involving images use the existing funding and readiness controls. Image content cannot grant new tool permissions or change the Tutor's operating instructions.
- [ ] Uploading for a session is distinct from explicitly saving a Portfolio entry; respect the agreed storage policy for unsaved session material.
- [ ] Browser scenarios use fictional workbook and art images, correct a deliberate interpretation error, exercise an unreadable image, and verify unauthorized asset access is denied.

**Demo:** Correct a misread workbook question and solve its first step; then show a paper-art image and discuss the confirmed details.

**Scope:** Workbook and art share one image-upload and confirmation path, keeping this a single manageable slice. Camera capture from another device and an in-browser painting suite are not required.
