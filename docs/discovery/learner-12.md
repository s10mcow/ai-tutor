# Interview with the 12-year-old learner

## Preferences shared on 2026-11-16

- Enjoys mountain biking, especially jumps and the feeling of freedom and being airborne.
- Sometimes becomes frustrated, bored, or irritated while doing maths. Wants someone alongside them to help and coach them through the work.
- Maths questions normally come from a workbook.
- Prefers a laptop, mentioning its large screen and microphone.

## Coaching and interaction choices confirmed

- Work through one small step together, then let the learner try. Provide examples when needed and let the learner ask to slow down, see an example, or try independently.
- Offer biking exploration and help with existing maths as separate choices. Connect biking to maths or science when the learner chooses.
- Let the learner speak or type, with spoken tutor replies and the problem and work remaining visible on the laptop screen.
- When an example clearly is not helping, try a different example. The learner wants the coach to respond to difficulty without requiring a forceful complaint.
- If frustration continues, offer a different example, an easier starting point, or a short break, with the learner choosing what would help.

## Workbook input

- The parent subsequently approved uploading pictures of workbook questions. The tutor should confirm its interpretation before helping; the learner's preferred way of taking or uploading the picture can be checked during a usability trial.

These preferences describe this learner separately from the [younger child's preferences](learner-10.md).
