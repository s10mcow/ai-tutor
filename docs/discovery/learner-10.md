# Interview with the 10-year-old learner

## Preferences shared on 2026-11-16

- Enjoys painting and other art activities because making pictures is fun.
- Finds maths boring and wants a more enjoyable way to learn it. Has not yet described a particular maths difficulty.
- Would use a computer and prefers speaking to the tutor.

## Coaching and activity choices confirmed

- Prefers short games as a way to make maths more enjoyable, selecting them over picture puzzles or mysteries.

These preferences describe this learner separately from the older child's preferences.
